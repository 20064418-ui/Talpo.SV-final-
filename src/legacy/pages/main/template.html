Top Destinations

El Boqueron

San Salvador
Restaurants, Hotels, Park

A huge crater on the San Salvador Volcano, a short drive from the capital. Walk the rim trail for views of the crater and the city.

Go in the morning for clearer views.

Tours

Ataco

Ahuachapán
Culture & Color

A colorful colonial town on the Ruta de las Flores, known for its murals, coffee farms and craft shops. Weekends are the liveliest.

Try the local coffee and ask about farm visits.

Tours

The Sunzal

La Libertad
Surf & Beach Clubs

A famous surf break on the Pacific coast, with beach clubs, relaxed restaurants and golden sunsets.

Pair it with El Tunco Beach in the Tour builder.

Tours

Coatepeque

Santa Ana
Lake & Volcanoes

A volcanic crater lake with calm blue water, lakeside restaurants and views of the surrounding volcanoes. Great for a relaxed day trip.

Pair it with Cerro Verde National Park, which overlooks the lake.
