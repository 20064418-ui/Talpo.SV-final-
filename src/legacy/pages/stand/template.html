EcoTrack 🌱

"Empowering communities to live sustainably, step by step."

When you arrive physically at any of our Picture Stands, you can scan the exclusive QR code at the location. This will open a quick interface where you'll be asked: How dirty is this place? In this way, EcoTrack and the local teams will receive real-time alerts to coordinate immediate cleaning and maintenance of the area.
